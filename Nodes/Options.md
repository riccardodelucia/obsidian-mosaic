Options are a [[derivative]] financial product, whose price is derived from another assets, called the **underlying**.

There are two types of options:
- PUT
- CALL

PUT options give options' buyer the right to *sell* a specific number of underlying units at a specific price, called **strike price**.

CALL options give options' buyer the right to *buy* a specific number of underlying units at a specific price, called **strike price**.

Options are intrinsically connected to the concept of [[Volatility]].
